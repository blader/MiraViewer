The first remaining implementation boundary was F3: separate source/window-dependent grayscale from marks, mask contours, crosshair and animation-frame-coalesced brush drafts; extend sparse changes through the label owner and durable writer. Its contract preserves original labels, exact hard marks, undo/redo, source ownership, failed-save recovery and sparse GPU updates, without a remount-losing debounce or unversioned shared mutable buffer. Acceptance compares long-gesture raster builds, whole-volume copies, written payload and pointer-to-paint work, then verifies the reopened result rather than merely a submitted save.

F3 ownership checkpoint: the initial copy-on-write design assumed that downstream heavy-operation preparation would always precede any retained label read. A direct callback regression disproved that assumption: requesting original detail, then editing again before the callback prepared its work, changed the callback's input. Keep the single editing owner, but borrow its current revision at the callback handoff itself. The alternative of copying every published mask preserves implicit snapshot semantics but retains the original per-stroke cost. Ordinary displays consume versioned views; history retains immutable patches; asynchronous readers receive borrowed revisions; storage captures its payload before yielding. A further legitimate reader that cannot obey this contract would reopen the representation decision, not justify an unrelated cache guard. The retained failing reproduction is `frontend/tmp/sparse-editing-borrow-handoff-before.log`; the corrected regression and acceptance below passed.

### Important details

**Source-owned settings.** Safe legacy rows are projected in memory during readonly hydration, not migrated by reads. The first intentional canonical save preserves legacy provenance; ambiguous rows still require explicit assignment. Unrelated imports do not replace the current patient's manual adjustments or browsing progress. Restore/recreation rotates the saved-work token, retiring old callbacks and rejecting queued/cross-tab writers atomically.

**Heavy-operation ownership.** Reconstruction, refinement, enhancement, selection, custom inference and model-file replacement all prepare through the imaging context. The caller's retained editing snapshot is counted as a floor, not added again to the current editor. The admitted runtime estimate is passed unchanged to the tracking owner; subsequent compressed prompts cannot lower it. Custom workers terminate on result, error, abort or deadline and are never retained. EfficientTAM sessions may be retained for 30 seconds, but each job gets fresh history and channel state.

**Exact versus blended display.** Exact mode is calibrated at the source plane, not a claim that the whole volume render is diagnostically calibrated. Unsupported samples remain absent, nearer opaque anatomy may occlude, and a selected contour can overlay the plane. Blend mode deliberately changes luminance for anatomical context. Invalid source identity clears content; an ordinary same-source decode failure preserves the last good plane with a notice.

**Memory evidence.** The new 128³ synthetic oracle contains 20 MiB of real weights used by a data-dependent Gather, not filler metadata or an unused initializer. It proves this model's execution and output, not arbitrary model complexity. Chromium memory dumps sample the worker-host renderer, including its page. They are neither isolated worker allocation nor a guaranteed instantaneous/OS-release peak. The admission policy is not an allocator cap.
